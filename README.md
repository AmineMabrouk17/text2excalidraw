## ✨ What is this?

**Text2Excalidraw** draws diagrams for you from your own words. Type something like:

> *"Architecture of Uber with Auth and PostgreSQL"*

…and it instantly draws a clean, editable diagram on a whiteboard. You can then
move the boxes, change the colors, rename things, and export the picture as a
PNG or SVG file — exactly like in [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com).

No drawing skills needed. No design software. Just describe what you want.

---

## 💡 What you can do

- **Describe anything, get a diagram.** Available in any language.
- **Move and redecorate.** Boxes and arrows are fully editable after the drawing appears.
- **Export and share.** Save your diagram as an image or use it in your documents and slides.
- **Works instantly.** No account, no setup, no key to paste — just open it and type.

---

## 🚀 How to use it (3 simple steps)

1. **Open the app:** [https://text2excalidraw.vercel.app](https://text2excalidraw.vercel.app)
2. **Type what you want** in the box, for example *"Login flow with email and two-factor authentication"*.
3. **Hit Generate** (or press Enter) and watch your diagram appear.

That's it. If you ever want, you can also bring your own AI key for even higher
limits — the app even shows you how.

---

## 📝 Example prompts

- *"Login flow with email, password and two-factor auth"*
- *"Online shop: cart, payments, inventory and shipping"*
- *"How a search engine works: crawler, index, ranking"*
- *"Class diagram for a library: Book, Member, Loan"*

The clearer your sentence, the better the diagram.

## 🔒 Privacy

- The free shared AI key is built into the site and **hidden** — it lives on
  our server, so you can use it freely but no one can see or steal it.
- If you add your own key, it's saved **only in your browser** and sent only to
  the AI provider you chose.
- Your ideas and descriptions stay yours.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Does it cost anything?** No. It's free to use.
